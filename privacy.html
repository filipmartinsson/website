Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

Filip Martinsson ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our iOS applications (each, an "App").

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with personalized AI-generated training programs, we may collect:

  • Profile Data: First name, age, gender, height, and weight.
  • Training Data: HYROX race and station times you enter, generated training plans, workouts, notes you write, and which workouts you complete.
  • Account Information: An account identifier and email address provided by Apple or Google when you sign in. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email," we only ever receive the relay address Apple generates, never your real one.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the App, including screens viewed and features used, together with app performance information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Generate and adapt personalized training programs using artificial intelligence.
  • Provide, maintain, and improve our applications.
  • Process subscription transactions and provide customer support.
  • Understand how our applications are used, in aggregate, so we can improve them.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Service Providers

We use a small number of third-party providers to operate our applications. They process data only on our instructions, and we do not sell your personal data to anyone.

  • Google (Gemini API): Your training preferences and, where you have provided them, your profile details and HYROX times are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate your training programs.
  • Supabase: Hosts our database and authentication.
  • RevenueCat: Manages subscription status. Payments themselves are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment details.
  • PostHog: Product analytics, hosted in the European Union, used to understand how our applications are used.

4. Data Shared Across Our Applications

We offer more than one training application. Because Apple and Google provide us with the same account identifier for all applications published by us, signing in to any of our applications with the same Apple or Google account gives you access to the same account.

Where that happens, information that describes you rather than a specific product — your profile details and your HYROX race times — is available across our applications. Training plans and workouts remain specific to the application that created them. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time (see Section 5).

5. Your GDPR Rights

We are based in Sweden, so your data is protected under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erase: Request deletion of your personal data. You can also delete your account and all associated data directly in the App, under Profile.
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent for us to process your data at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@filipmartinsson.com.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, or as required by law. Deleting your account removes your profile, training plans, workouts, and race times.

7. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
