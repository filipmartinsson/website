Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 9, 2026

Filip Martinsson AB ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our iOS application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with personalized AI-generated training programs, we may collect the following information:

  • Profile Data: Age, gender, height, weight, and fitness level.
  • Usage Data: Details about your interactions with the App, workouts completed, and app performance metrics.
  • Account Information: If you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and login credentials.

2. Apple Health App (HealthKit) Integration

Our App may request access to read and/or write data to the Apple Health App (HealthKit).

  • Consent: We will only access HealthKit data if you explicitly grant us permission through the iOS permissions prompt.
  • Use of Health Data: HealthKit data is used exclusively to tailor your AI training programs and track your fitness progress.
  • No Third-Party Marketing: We do not use HealthKit data for marketing or advertising purposes, and we do not sell this data to third parties.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Generate personalized training programs using Artificial Intelligence.
  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App's functionality.
  • Process subscription transactions and provide customer support.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Sharing and AI Processors

To generate your training programs, we may securely transmit anonymized or necessary profile data to third-party AI service providers (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic). We ensure these providers comply with strict data protection standards and only use the data to generate your requested workouts. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.

5. Your GDPR Rights

Because we are based in Sweden, your data is protected under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erase: Request deletion of your personal data ("Right to be Forgotten").
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent for us to process your data (including HealthKit access) at any time.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, or as required by law.
