Stretch Lock Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email hello@filipmartinsson.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days.

Common questions

My apps didn't lock. Check that Screen Time access is allowed for Stretch Lock in Settings › Screen Time, that the lock is on under Lock settings in the app, and that the lock time has passed. Apps only lock on days you haven't stretched yet.

"Start stretching" on a locked app does nothing. It works through a notification. Allow notifications for Stretch Lock in Settings › Notifications.

How do I unlock without stretching? Open Stretch Lock, go to Lock settings and turn the lock off.

How do I cancel my subscription? In Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or in the App Store under your account. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it.

I got a new phone. Tap Restore on the subscription screen to get your subscription back. Your lock settings and streak are stored on the phone, so you'll set the lock up again.
