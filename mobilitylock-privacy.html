Mobility Lock Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

Filip Martinsson ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Mobility Lock iOS app (the "App") uses, where it is kept, and what we receive. Our other apps have their own privacy policy.

In short: the App has no accounts and keeps your answers, your stretching history and the apps you lock on your phone. We receive anonymous information about how the App is used, and Apple and RevenueCat tell us about subscriptions. We never see which apps you lock or how you use them, we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we never sell your data.

1. Information That Stays on Your Phone

The following is stored only on your device. We don't receive it, and deleting the App deletes it:

  • Your name, if you enter it, and how you are feeling when you start.
  • Your stretching history: the days you stretched and your streak.
  • Your lock settings: the apps and websites you chose to lock and your lock time.

2. Screen Time and the Apps You Lock

The App uses Apple's Screen Time framework to lock the apps you choose until you have stretched. Apple gives the App only anonymous tokens for the apps, categories and websites you pick. The App cannot read their names or see what you do in them, how long you use them, your messages or your browsing. These tokens never leave your phone.

3. Information We Receive

  • Usage Data: How you use the App, such as screens viewed, onboarding steps, when you start and finish a stretching routine, your streak, whether you turned the lock on, how many apps you chose to lock (not which ones), and whether you allowed Screen Time access and notifications.
  • Onboarding Answers: The answers you give when setting up the App, used to build your routine and to understand who uses it: the body areas you want to stretch, whether you sit most of the day, the routine length and days per week you chose, how many hours a day you spend on your phone, your goals and what gets in their way, your relationship with your body, how committed you are, and your sex if you choose to give it.
  • Device and App Information: A random identifier created for your installation of the App, your device model, iOS version, App version, language, and an approximate location (country and city) estimated from your IP address.
  • Purchase Information: Which subscription you chose, when a free trial starts, converts or renews, and the price paid. Payments themselves are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment details. If you found the App through an Apple Search Ads advertisement, Apple may tell us which campaign it was. This does not use your advertising identifier and needs no tracking permission.

This information is linked to the random identifier for your installation, not to your name, email address or Apple ID.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Build your stretching routine and run the lock.
  • Provide subscriptions and customer support.
  • Understand how the App is used, in aggregate, so we can improve it.
  • Understand which of our advertising brings people to the App.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

5. Service Providers

We use two providers. They process data only on our instructions, and we do not sell your personal data to anyone.

  • PostHog: Product analytics, hosted in the European Union. Receives the usage data, onboarding answers and device information described above.
  • RevenueCat: Manages subscription status. Receives purchase information and the App's PostHog identifier, so subscriptions can be counted in our analytics.

6. No Tracking or Advertising Software

The App doesn't contain advertising or ad-measurement software from Meta, Google or anyone else, doesn't access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

7. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App shows them from your phone, for example when you tap "Start stretching" on a locked app or before a free trial ends. They are not sent through a server and nothing about them is sent to us.

8. Your GDPR Rights

We are based in Sweden, so your data is protected under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We process usage data and onboarding answers on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the App, and purchase information to provide the subscription you bought. You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erase: Request deletion of your personal data. Deleting the App removes everything stored on your phone.
  • Object: Object to our use of your data for analytics.

Because the App has no accounts, the data we receive isn't connected to your name or email. If you contact us, we'll help you find and delete the data for your installation. Contact us at hello@filipmartinsson.com.

9. Data Retention

We keep the data we receive only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, or as required by law.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
